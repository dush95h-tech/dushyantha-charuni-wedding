# Dushyantha & Charuni — Wedding invitation

An ivory-and-gold invitation with emerald lettering, traditional ornaments, a ten-second silent opening video, countdown and venue directions. No RSVP or music.

- index.html: wedding information and page structure
- kandyan.css: responsive styles and motion
- script.js: countdown and video controls, reduced-motion and playback-error fallback
- our-portrait.jpeg: user-supplied wedding illustration, unmodified
- our-invitation-10sec.mp4: 720 x 960, 24 fps H.264, exactly 10 seconds, silent
- ARTWORK.md: artwork provenance

Video supports pause, skip, replay and download. Published with GitHub Pages from main, root folder.

Wedding: 4 May 2027, 9:00 AM–3:30 PM Sri Lanka time. Poruwa ceremony: 10:30 AM. Pabavee Regency, Gampaha.
